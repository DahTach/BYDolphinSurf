Approved Zero-Damage Chemicals Cheat-Sheet

ASTM D471 Verified

1. Directly ON Non-Oil-Resistant EPDM Rubber <1–2% Swell

  • ✅ DuPont Krytox GPL 205 (PFPE): 100% inert fluorinated grease (<1% swell on all rubbers).
  • ✅ DuPont Molykote 111 Silicone Paste: Pure PDMS paste in a tube (<2% EPDM swell; blocks winter ice).
  • ✅ Pure Ph. Eur. Vegetable Glycerine / Sonax GummiPfleger: Polar alcohol; zero EPDM swell, pharmacy-grade.
  • ❌ NEVER USE: WD-40, Vaseline (petroleum jelly), mineral oil, or cheap aerosol silicone sprays (solvent carriers cause 30–80% EPDM swelling).

2. Underbody & Sill Rustproofers (Rubber-Safe) 70kV Dielectric

  • ✅ Lanoguard (Zero-Petroleum Lanolin): Solvent-free wool grease; safe on all rubbers, plastics & HV wiring.
  • ✅ Dinitrol 3125 / ML (Cavities) & 4941 (Underbody): Curing elastic synthetic wax; harms zero rubber/plastics once dry.
  • ⚠️ EV BOUNDARY RULES: Avoid Fluid Film or thin petroleum-carrier oils near soft EPDM seals. Never coat brake discs, orange HV connectors, or the flat aluminum Blade Battery bottom plate (acts as thermal blanket).

DOLPHIN SURF COMFORT (2025) — 15-YR / 300k KM CHEAT-SHEET

43.2 kWh Blade LFP • 115 kW / 220 Nm • N. Italy Outdoor Salt & 10% Pothole Spec

ODO: 0 km
Severe Schedule Active
🟢 1. Do Once When New (Pre-Winter)

1. Front & Rear Mud Flaps

Clip-on flaps; stops winter salt/gravel sandblasting lower sills.

2. Underbody & Sill Cavity Wax

Lanoguard or Dinitrol 3125/4941 inside sill bungs & steel subframes/arms. Mask brakes, HV plugs & flat aluminum battery belly plate.

3. Condition All EPDM Seals

Coat charge flap ring, door & frunk rubbers with Molykote 111, Krytox GPL 205, or Pure Glycerine.

4. 3m / 5,000 km Check

Official BYD initial chassis bolt & HV shield settling check.

🔵 2. Routine DIY & Severe Annual

5. Monthly: 100% LFP Balance + PSI

Charge to 100% AC + leave plugged in 1–2 hrs to top-balance LFP cells.

6. Every 6m: Charge Port & Flap Ring

Wipe pins w/ 99% IPA; re-lube outer flap seal w/ Molykote 111 / Glycerine (stops green oxide & compression set).

7. Every 10,000 km: Tire Rotation

Front-to-back + annual alignment check after winter potholes.

8. Every 12m / 15,000 km (Severe Rule)

Overrides 24m/30k standard EU rule. Lube caliper guide pins + weekly firm braking; inspect drop links, tie rods & plastic aero shield; touch up Lanoguard.

🟠 3. Hard Workshop Fluid Milestones

9. 24m / 40k km: Gearbox Oil Flush

Spec: Castrol BOT384 (450–600 ml). Repeat every 24m / 48,000 km. Flushes break-in metal shavings to save 8-in-1 bearings.

10. Every 24m: Brake Fluid Flush

Spec: HZY6 / DOT 4 (~1,100 ml). Strictly time-based to prevent moisture corrosion inside ABS/regen valves.

11. Every 36m / 60–80k km: Coolant & 12V

Spec: BYD Glycol OAT -40°C (3.1–3.3 L). Earlier than 4yr/100k manual limit to protect electric pumps. Test/replace 12V battery every 3–4 yrs.

🛡️ SAFE UNDERBODY RUSTPROOFERS: 1) Lanoguard (solvent-free lanolin, 70kV safe) • 2) Dinitrol 3125 / 4941. (❌ NO Fluid Film on EPDM; NEVER coat flat battery heat-sink plate).
